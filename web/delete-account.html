Delete your account

You can permanently delete your HabitFlow account and all of its data.

In the app

Profile → Delete account. This removes your habits, check-ins, journal entries, Coach history, progress and device tokens from our servers. It cannot be undone.

Cancelling a subscription is separate: delete your account after cancelling in your Apple ID settings, or you may keep being billed.

Can't open the app?

Email [your support email] from the address on your account (or tell us the Apple sign-in you used) with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your data and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.
