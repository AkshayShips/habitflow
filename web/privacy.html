What we collect

  • Account: your email address (magic link) or the private relay address and name Apple provides if you use Sign in with Apple. Guest accounts store no email.
  • Your content: habits, check-ins, notes, moods, journal entries, and your time zone, so your days roll over at the right time.
  • AI Coach: messages you send to the Coach and its replies, plus a short summary of your habit data needed to answer. Coach requests are processed by our AI provider, Anthropic.
  • Device and usage: push notification token, app version, crash reports, and product analytics events (for example, "habit completed").
  • Purchases: subscription status, handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads (free plan): an advertising partner may show ads. We ask permission before any tracking, and you can decline.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your data between devices, send reminders you set, provide the AI Coach, process subscriptions, fix crashes, and understand which features help people. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, protected by per-user access rules so each account can only read its own rows. Habit data is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: delete your account and all associated data from within the app, or request it here.
  • Export: export your data as CSV from within the app.
  • Access or correct: contact us below. Depending on where you live (for example the UK/EU under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights, and we honor them.
  • Tracking: you can change the tracking permission any time in iOS Settings.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account, except where law requires us to keep limited records (such as purchase records).

Children

HabitFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
